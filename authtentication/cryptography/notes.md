### **What is Cryptography?**

Cryptography is the practice of securing information by converting it into a coded format, so that only authorized parties can access or understand it. It plays a crucial role in protecting the privacy, integrity, and authenticity of data in digital communication.

A **key** is used in cryptography to encrypt (lock) and decrypt (unlock) information. The type of key and how it is used determines the kind of cryptography being applied.

---

### **Types of Cryptography:**

1. **Symmetric Key Cryptography**

   - Uses a **single key** for both encryption and decryption.
   - This key is shared between the sender and the receiver.
   - Example: **AES (Advanced Encryption Standard)**
   - Often used for **session-based communication**.

2. **Asymmetric Key Cryptography**

   - Uses **two keys**: a **public key** and a **private key**.
   - The public key is used to encrypt data, and the private key is used to decrypt it (or vice versa depending on the use case).
   - Example: **RSA (Rivest–Shamir–Adleman)**
   - Commonly used for **secure data transmission**, **digital signatures**, and **authentication**.
